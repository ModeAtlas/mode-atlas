Kanji learning sources

Mode Atlas uses readings, meanings and vocabulary from OpenJLPT, including JMdict and KANJIDIC2, copyright James William Breen and the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group. Approximate level assignments come from Jonathan Waller’s study lists.

The adapted learning dataset is available under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0.

Stroke-order artwork

Kanji and kana stroke paths come from KanjiVG, copyright Ulrich Apel and contributors, under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 3.0. Mode Atlas extracts the ordered paths and groups them by study band, preserving their original geometry.
