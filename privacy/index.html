Privacy Policy

Updated 30 September 2026. Mode Atlas is developed by Jack Wright. This policy covers the Mode Atlas website and iOS app. Contact support@mode-atlas.com about privacy or your account.

What stays on your device

You can practise without an account. Your answers, learning totals, settings, practice sessions and Word Bank are saved on your device. Clearing browser storage or deleting the app can remove this local save. Signing out does not itself erase your local learning save.

Accounts and cloud saves

If you sign in, Google Firebase Authentication provides your account identifier, email address, display name, sign-in provider and available profile photo. Mode Atlas does not receive your sign-in provider’s password. Available sign-in methods are shown in Profile.

Your learning progress, practice settings, results, goals, achievements, saved words and notes are synced to Google Cloud Firestore under your account. We use this information to save and restore your progress and provide the features you choose. Guest progress on the device may be combined with your account when you sign in. A random installation identifier helps combine progress without counting the same work twice. When you switch accounts, an account-specific local cache preserves offline progress separately.

Friends is optional

Creating a Friends profile shares your chosen display name, avatar, level, title and frame with people who have your friend code. Accepted friends can also see your study streak, mastery and learning totals in Friends and rankings. Your email address, private notes and sign-in details are not shown in Friends.

You can choose an Atlas symbol, an emoji, or your linked Google account photo. Account photos are loaded from Google. Friend codes, requests, connections and blocked profiles are stored to provide these features. Leave Friends to remove your Friends profile and connections while keeping your learning account.

Reports and safety

When you report a profile, we store your account identifier, the reported profile, a snapshot of its name and avatar, your reason and any details you provide. The moderation team can review these reports and reset or restrict profiles. The person you report does not receive your identity. Reports expire after 90 days, with deletion handled by the database’s expiry process. Restrictions remain until removed or the account is deleted.

Reminders and widgets

Study reminders are scheduled locally on your device after you grant notification permission. You can turn them off in Settings or iPhone Settings. Widgets read a local snapshot of your learning totals and last activity from the app’s shared storage; this snapshot does not include your name, email or private words. Signing out or resetting data clears the widget snapshot and reminder preferences.

Service providers and diagnostics

Mode Atlas uses Google Firebase for sign-in, cloud storage and server functions. These services process technical information such as requests, network addresses and service logs to operate and protect the service. Cloud providers may process information outside your country. Their handling is described in Firebase’s privacy information.

Mode Atlas does not run advertising or track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If you choose Send feedback, your email app opens a draft with the app version, platform and current screen; you decide what to send. TestFlight feedback and crash information are handled through Apple’s beta-testing service when you use TestFlight.

Retention and deletion

Your cloud save remains while your account exists. Reset data removes learning progress but keeps your sign-in account. Delete account & data in Settings asks for confirmation and a fresh sign-in, then removes the sign-in account, cloud learning data and Friends information. If cleanup is interrupted after the account is deleted, the server retries it. Other devices may retain local copies until you clear their data; downloaded backups and messages you have sent separately are not recalled.

Deletion access barriers are kept for seven days to prevent previously issued sign-in tokens from recreating data, then expire automatically. Short-lived request-limit records expire after one day. Service-provider logs and backups can follow the provider’s own retention schedule. Contact us if you need help accessing, correcting or deleting information, or if you cannot use the in-app deletion option.

Changes and questions

We update this page when data practices change. The date above identifies this version. For questions, a privacy request or a concern about a child’s information, email support@mode-atlas.com.
